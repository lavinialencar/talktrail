Talktrail: complemento da pasta ao vivo
=========================================

O que é: um programinha local que salva cada reunião em Documentos/Talktrail,
em tempo real, uma subpasta por mês. Tudo fica no seu computador.

Como instalar (uma vez só):
  Mac:      abra "instalar-mac.command" (botão direito > Abrir, na primeira vez)
  Windows:  abra "instalar-windows.bat" (se o Windows avisar, "Mais informações" > "Executar assim mesmo")
  Linux:    rode "bash instalar-linux.sh"

Precisa do Python 3; o instalador avisa e abre a página de download se faltar.

Pra desinstalar: apague a pasta "Talktrail" de
  Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support   e   ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.talktrail.server.plist
  Windows:  %LOCALAPPDATA%   e o atalho Talktrail.vbs da pasta Inicializar
  Linux:    ~/.local/share/talktrail   e   ~/.config/autostart/talktrail.desktop

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Talktrail: live folder add-on (English)
=======================================

What it is: a small local program that saves each meeting to Documents/Talktrail
in real time, with one subfolder per month. Everything stays on your computer.
It is optional: the extension also works without it.

How to install (once):
  Mac:      open "instalar-mac.command" (right-click > Open, the first time)
  Windows:  open "instalar-windows.bat" (if Windows warns you, "More info" > "Run anyway")
  Linux:    run "bash instalar-linux.sh"

It needs Python 3; the installer tells you and opens the download page if it is missing.

To uninstall, delete the "Talktrail" folder from
  Mac:      ~/Library/Application Support   and   ~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.talktrail.server.plist
  Windows:  %LOCALAPPDATA%   and the Talktrail.vbs shortcut in the Startup folder
  Linux:    ~/.local/share/talktrail   and   ~/.config/autostart/talktrail.desktop
